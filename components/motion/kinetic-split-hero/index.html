Reusable behavior

Keep the stack. Focus one layer.

All three cards remain visible in the stepped composition. Hover, keyboard focus, or tap brings one card forward and reveals its supporting content while the other layers stay in place.

Build this interaction

Prompt

Create a premium three-card kinetic hero for a modern product page. Use a restrained dark palette with one vivid accent and a clear two-column desktop composition: editorial hero copy on the left and three visibly stepped cards on the right. Keep all three cards visible at the same time so the layered concept is obvious immediately.

Each card should show only its number, short label, and visual treatment in the resting state. When a user hovers, keyboard-focuses, or taps a card, make the motion clearly visible: bring that card above the other layers, lift it roughly 50–70px, move it forward in depth, scale it to about 1.06–1.08, straighten most of its resting rotation, deepen the shadow, and slightly shift/scale the background artwork. At the same time, reveal the supporting eyebrow, headline, and description by fading and sliding the copy upward around 24–32px. The other two cards must remain visible and stay in their original positions so the stack still reads as one composition.

Use CSS transitions for the primary hover/focus motion with a refined custom easing curve. Use only a very small vanilla JavaScript helper to support persistent tap state on coarse-pointer devices. Do not use scroll-driven animation, pinning, or scroll hijacking for this component. Respect prefers-reduced-motion by revealing the content without transform animation. On mobile, stack the three cards vertically and keep the full content readable without hover. Include visible keyboard focus states and preserve all essential content if JavaScript fails.
