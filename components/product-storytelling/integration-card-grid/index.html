Structure: four equal cards, each with a unique abstract symbol, heading, description, and CTA.
Motion: slight card elevation, icon shift, and CTA arrow movement on hover/focus.
Responsive: 4 columns on large screens, 2 on tablet, 1 on mobile.
Accessibility: no hover-only content, focus-visible links, reduced-motion fallback.
Constraints: original generic symbols built in CSS; no third-party logos or images.
