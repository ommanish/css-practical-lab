06 / Scroll

The quiet power of progress.

A long-form reading layout with a CSS scroll timeline.

01

Orientation matters.

Long pages feel easier when readers can sense how far they have travelled. A subtle progress rail provides context without interrupting the content.

02

Let the document drive it.

Modern scroll timelines can connect animation progress to the page itself, avoiding a scroll listener for simple visual feedback.

03

Motion should stay secondary.

The indicator is useful, but the article remains completely readable when animation is unavailable or reduced.
